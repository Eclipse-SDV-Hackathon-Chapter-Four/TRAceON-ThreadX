Single logs:

  ./scripts/send-log.sh DLT_WARN  SensorTask 'humidity sensor returned no data'
  ./scripts/send-log.sh DLT_ERROR MQTT       'broker ping timeout'

Watch the SINK pane for forwarded entries and the CONTROL pane for the rising
'forwarded' counter.
